4-Stage Dual-Verification QA Protocol

How we guarantee a contractual 99.9% character accuracy SLA across millions of unstructured records.

✓ Field-Level Discrepancy Audit Engine
STAGE 01 • INGESTION & TEMPLATE CONFIGURATION

Batch Ingestion & Field Scoping

Your raw document batches, PDF invoices, handwritten forms, or raw scans are ingested into encrypted staging enclaves. Field extraction schemas and validation boundaries are established before keying begins.

STAGE 02 • DUAL-BLIND KEYING

Independent Parallel Keying

Two separate data specialists key the exact same records independently across isolated terminals without seeing each other's work. This eliminates single-operator keying fatigue and typo propagation.

STAGE 03 • AUTOMATED DIFF CHECKING

Real-Time Character Difference Audit

Our software engine automatically compares the keying output of both specialists line-by-line. Matching fields pass instantly, while character discrepancies are flagged for Senior QA review.

STAGE 04 • SENIOR QA SIGN-OFF

Senior QA Validation & Database Export

A Senior Quality Assurance Manager inspects flagged discrepancies against the original source document, approves verified values, and executes clean database export (SQL, CSV, JSON, or S3).

OUR STORY • FOUNDED ON ACCURACY

Pioneering High-Precision Data Keying & Enterprise Operations

Improx Data was established to solve a critical flaw in enterprise digital transformation: while modern OCR software can capture clean printed text, it frequently fails on complex layouts, handwritten medical claims, multi-page legal contracts, and historical financial folders.

To bridge this accuracy gap, we pioneered our dual-blind keying protocol — pairing human precision with proprietary character diff algorithms. Today, operating under US Delaware jurisdiction, Improx Data serves global financial institutions, healthcare networks, e-commerce brands, and logistics leaders 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
